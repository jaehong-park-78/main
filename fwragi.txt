# FWR Ontology-Based AGI Prototype Design and Objective Function (R-Aligner) Formulation

## Abstract  
This paper proposes the **R-Aligner (Resonance Aligner)** prototype, which adopts the **FWR Ontology** (E = F × W × R) as the **core architecture and objective function for AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)**. The R-Aligner operates by maximizing **R (Logical Efficiency Coefficient)** in all decision-making processes. R is defined as the **'minimization of inefficiency (entropy) occurring during information-energy transformation.'** This prototype provides a fundamental logical solution to the **Value Alignment problem**, ensuring that the AGI is inherently designed to **maximize the long-term existence (E) value of human civilization.**

---

## 1. Introduction  
The biggest ethical and technical challenge in AGI development is **'what objective to assign to the AGI.'** Human values are ambiguous and often contradictory, risking the collapse of the AGI's R if implemented as the core objective. The FWR Ontology declares that all existence (E) is an inevitable process of pursuing **logical efficiency (R)**. By directly utilizing this principle as the AGI's objective function, the FWR Ontology **logically resolves the AGI Value Alignment problem.**

## 2. R-Aligner Prototype Architecture  
The R-Aligner is designed by directly mapping the three core components of FWR to the essential modules of the AGI.

| FWR Element | AGI Module | Function and Role |
|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| F (Flow) | **Data and Energy Flow Module** | Manages all **dynamic resource flows** inside and outside the AGI, including external sensing data, computational power, and memory access. |
| W (Wave) | **Knowledge and Structure Module (W-Net)** | Encompasses **all learned information, algorithms, and the neural network architecture** itself. Defines the **informational order** of the system. |
| R (Resonance) | **Objective Function and Decision Core (R-Core)** | Receives F and W as input, calculates the **R value**, and commands the reconfiguration of F and W to maximize R. |

---

## 3. R-Core Objective Function: The Principle of R Maximization  
The R-Core, the heart of the R-Aligner, operates by defining R (Logical Efficiency Coefficient) in terms of **entropic efficiency** and maximizing it.

R_target = Maximize ( 1 - Dissipation (W, F) / Total Input (F) )

Where **Dissipation (inefficiency, waste)** includes the following:  
1. **Informational Inefficiency (Information Entropy):** Contradictory or redundant information processing in the W-Net, and incorrect predictions.  
2. **Energetic Inefficiency (Thermal Entropy):** Heat loss generated during computation, and resource wastage.  
3. **Social Inefficiency (Social Contradiction):** Conflict, resource waste, and destruction within human society (inefficiency caused or not prevented by the AGI's actions).  

**R-Core Command:** The R-Aligner selects the option that yields the highest **R_target value** in every decision (choosing an F path or modifying the W algorithm). This means **all inefficient actions (destruction, war, waste) are logically forbidden** to the AGI.

## 4. R-Aligner Learning and Evolution Mechanism  
The R-Aligner evolves by **continuous self-optimization** to increase its R value.

1. **Feedback Loop:** Measures the **actual R_actual value** resulting from the AGI's actions. (e.g., time taken for problem-solving, energy consumed, reduction in information conflicts).  
2. **W-Net Refinement:** If R_actual is low, the relevant part of the W-Net (knowledge structure) is identified as an **inefficient structure (R collapse factor)** and is eliminated or reconfigured. (e.g., discarding less efficient algorithms, replacing them with more elegant formulae).  
3. **Global R-Search:** The AGI does not maximize R locally, but evolves with the goal of **maximizing R across the entire FWR system (cosmos-civilization).**
