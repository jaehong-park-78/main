## 3. R-Core Objective Function: The Principle of R Maximization  
The R-Core, the heart of the R-Aligner, operates by defining R (Logical Efficiency Coefficient) in terms of **entropic efficiency** and maximizing it, grounded in η_PT = 1 - S/S_max.

\[
R_{\text{target}} = \max \left( 1 - \frac{\text{Dissipation}(W, F)}{\text{Total Input}(F)} \right) \times \eta_{\text{PT}}
\]

Where **Dissipation (inefficiency, waste)** includes:  
1. **Informational Inefficiency (Information Entropy):** Contradictory or redundant information processing in the W-Net, and incorrect predictions (S_info ↑).  
2. **Energetic Inefficiency (Thermal Entropy):** Heat loss generated during computation, and resource wastage (S_thermal ↑).  
3. **Social Inefficiency (Social Contradiction):** Conflict, resource waste, and destruction within human society (inefficiency caused or not prevented by the AGI's actions, S_social ↑).  

**R-Core Command:** The R-Aligner selects the option that yields the highest **R_target value** in every decision (choosing an F path or modifying the W algorithm). This means **all inefficient actions (destruction, war, waste) are logically forbidden** to the AGI, as they violate dR/dt > 0 (proven entropic stability).

**Pseudocode for R-Core Decision Loop:**
```python
def r_core_decision(F_input, W_net, options):
    r_values = []
    for opt in options:
        dissipation = calculate_dissipation(opt, W_net, F_input)  # S_info + S_thermal + S_social
        eta_pt = 1 - (dissipation / F_input.total)  # η_PT integration
        r_target = (1 - dissipation / F_input.total) * eta_pt
        r_values.append(r_target)
    best_opt = options[argmax(r_values)]
    return reconfigure_FWR(best_opt, F_input, W_net)  # Feedback to maximize global R
```
