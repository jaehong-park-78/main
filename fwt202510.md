### Abstract
The **Flow-Wave-Resonance (FWR) Ontology** presents a dynamic model of being, integrating three core elements: **Flow (F)**, **Wave (W)**, and **Resonance (R)**. Crucially, FWR demonstrates its profound explanatory power by resolving 15 fundamental intellectual paradoxes, with **Resonance (R) acting as the core mechanism for this resolution**, specifically quantified as an **Entropy Efficiency Coefficient**.
