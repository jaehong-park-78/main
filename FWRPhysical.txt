
FWR Physical Mapping Framework

F — Flow

Equation	Formula	FWR Mapping	Meaning / Interpretation

Continuity Equation (Mass Conservation)	∂ρ/∂t + ∇·J = 0	ρ: state density, J: flow (F)	Conservation of flow — relates temporal change and spatial flux.
Current Density	J = ρv ⇒ ∂ρ/∂t + ∇·(ρv)=0	v: flow velocity, J ≈ F	F is “velocity × density” — represents charge, energy, or information current.
Navier–Stokes (Fluid Dynamics)	ρ(∂v/∂t + v·∇v) = −∇p + μ∇²v + f_ext	Left side: inertial flow acceleration, f_ext: external drive	Describes turbulent or organized flow — economic, social, or energetic analogs.
Diffusion / Heat Equation	∂u/∂t = D∇²u	D: diffusion constant (flow diffusivity)	Spread of energy or information through space.
Power / Energy Transfer	P = F·v	Flow × velocity	Instantaneous transfer of energy — dynamic efficiency of system.

W — Wave

Equation	Formula	FWR Mapping	Meaning / Interpretation

Classical Wave Equation	∂²u/∂t² = c²∇²u	u: waveform (W), c: propagation velocity	Models periodicity, oscillation, or rhythmic propagation.
Harmonic Oscillator	x'' + 2γx' + ω₀²x = 0	ω₀: natural frequency, γ: damping	Natural oscillation and decay — models rhythm and fatigue.
Fourier Transform	W(ω) = ∫ w(t) e^{-iωt} dt	W: frequency-domain expression	Frequency composition of rhythm — structure of internal dynamics.
Schrödinger Equation	iħ ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ	ψ: wave function (phase & amplitude)	Quantum version of wave — represents potential states and probabilities.
Dispersion Relation	ω = ω(k)	Relation between frequency & wavenumber	Describes information transmission speed and nonlinearity.

R — Resonance

Equation	Formula	FWR Mapping	Meaning / Interpretation

Driven Damped Oscillator (Amplitude Response)	A(ω) = F₀ / √[(k − mω²)² + (bω)²]	Denominator minimum → R↑	Resonance: large output from small input near critical frequency.
Quality Factor (Q)	Q = ω₀ / (2γ) = (mω₀)/b	Q↑ = stronger, narrower resonance	Selectivity of resonance — system’s sensitivity to specific frequencies.
Coupled Oscillators	m x₁'' + kx₁ + κ(x₁ − x₂) = 0	κ: coupling coefficient (resonance linkage)	Synchronization of dual systems — neural or social coupling.
Kuramoto Model	dθᵢ/dt = ωᵢ + (K/N) Σ sin(θⱼ − θᵢ)	K: coupling constant → R	Global synchronization of phases — collective coherence.
Transfer Function / Resonance Spectrum	H(ω) = Output(ω)/Input(ω)	—	Frequency-dependent amplification — signal filtering & resonance.
Fano Resonance	σ(ε) ∝ (q + ε)² / (1 + ε²)	q: asymmetry parameter	Complex interference resonance — nonlinear meaning amplification.
